# PART 2 — CUSTOM (HOMEBREW) SPELL DESCRIPTIONS
(All ranges/areas pre-converted to metric. Components are V, S unless noted. "At Higher Levels" = source scaling, verbatim.)

## WIND — Aero family
**Aero** — Evocation cantrip. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. A burst of wind around a creature within range. The target makes a Dexterity save or takes 1d8 thunder damage; it gains no benefit from cover for this save. Higher: damage +1d8 at 5th (2d8), 11th (3d8), 17th (4d8).
**Aerora (Aero II)** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. A raging vacuum (3 m radius sphere) at a point in range disperses gas/vapor, extinguishes unprotected flames, and has a 50% chance to extinguish protected flames. Creatures in the area make a Dexterity save or take 3d6 thunder (half on success). Higher: +1d6 per slot above 2nd.
**Aeroga (Aero III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. A windstorm in a 6 m radius sphere. Creatures make a Strength save or take 5d8 thunder and are knocked 6 m straight back from center (half, no knockback on success). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 3rd (6th+).
**Aeroja (Aero IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. A vortex in a 6 m radius, 12 m high cylinder. On entering for the first time on a turn or starting there: Strength save or 3d6 thunder + 3d6 slashing (half on success). The area is difficult terrain. Bonus action to move the vortex 18 m. Higher: +2d6 thunder per slot above 5th.

## ICE — Blizzard family
**Blizzard** — Evocation cantrip. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Ice aether around a target: Dexterity save or 1d8 cold and speed -3 m until the start of your next turn (no cover benefit). Higher: +1d8 at 5th/11th/17th.
**Blizzara (Blizzard II)** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range Self; Concentration up to 1 minute. A 3 m radius circle of ice around you. Creatures make a Dexterity save or take 3d6 cold and are restrained (half, not restrained on success). A restrained creature can use its action for a Strength check vs your spell save DC to free itself. Higher: +1d6 per slot above 1st.
**Blizzaga (Blizzard III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. A localized blizzard, 6 m radius sphere: Constitution save, 6d6 cold (half on success). The ground becomes slick ice for 1 minute or until melted (moving more than half speed across it requires a DC 15 Acrobatics check at the start of the move or fall prone). Higher: +1d6 per slot above 3rd.
**Blizzaja (Blizzard IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. A massive ice shard falls and explodes; creatures within 9 m radius sphere make a Dexterity save or take 7d8 cold. The ground becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute or until melted. Higher: +1d8 per slot above 5th.

## FIRE — Fire family
**Fire** — Evocation cantrip. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Ranged spell attack; on a hit, 2d4 fire. A flammable unattended object ignites. Higher: +2d4 at 5th (4d4), 11th (6d4), 17th (8d4).
**Fira (Fire II)** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Ranged spell attack; on a hit, 1d10 fire. Hit or miss, it explodes: the target and creatures within 1.5 m make a Dexterity save or take 2d6 fire. Higher: secondary +1d6 per slot above 1st.
**Firaga (Fire III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. Target makes a Dexterity save or takes 5d8 fire and burns for the duration (half, no burn on success). At the start of its turn it takes 1d8 fire; at the end of its turn it repeats the save, ending on success. Higher: burning +1d6 per slot above 3rd.
**Firaja (Fire IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. A spherical cage of fire, 6 m radius, at a point in range. Creatures inside make a Dexterity save or take 4d8 fire (half on success). The cage persists; as an action you can force all inside to re-save for 3d8 fire (half on success). Leaving through the flames deals 4d8 fire. Higher: initial +1d8 per slot above 5th.

## LIGHTNING — Thunder family
**Thunder** — Evocation cantrip. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Ranged spell attack; on a hit, 2d6 lightning. Higher: +1d6 at 5th (3d6), 11th (4d6), 17th (5d6).
**Thundara (Thunder II)** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. A bolt at one target; three bolts then leap to up to three others within 9 m of the first (each target hit by only one bolt). Each makes a Dexterity save, 2d8 lightning (half on success). Higher: +1 leaping bolt per slot above 2nd.
**Thundaga (Thunder III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. Ranged spell attack; on a hit, 4d8 lightning, then explodes in a 3 m radius sphere: Dexterity save, 4d8 lightning (half on success). Higher: explosion +1d8 per slot above 3rd.
**Thundaja (Thunder IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. An orb of electricity hovers in place. A hostile creature moving within 6 m of it for the first time on a turn makes a Dexterity save, 20 lightning (half on success). The orb vanishes after dealing 80 total damage. Higher: +20 max total per slot above 5th.

## EARTH — Stone family
**Stone** — Evocation cantrip. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Ranged spell attack; on a hit, 1d8 bludgeoning and speed -3 m until the start of your next turn. Higher: +1d8 at 5th/11th/17th.
**Stonra (Stone II)** — 1st transmutation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Stalagmites burst up; creatures in a 1.5 m radius circle make a Dexterity save or take 3d8 bludgeoning and are knocked prone (half, not prone on success). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 1st.
**Stonga (Stone III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. Create 4 stone spears converging in a straight line on a point in range. Any creature between a spear and the convergence point makes a Dexterity save or takes 2d6 bludgeoning (half on success). Higher: +1 spear per slot above 3rd.
**Stonja (Stone IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. Raise a 6 m square of land 6 m into the air; choose the speed. Slow = creatures harmlessly lifted; Fast = creatures make a Constitution save or take 6d6 bludgeoning and are knocked prone (half, no effect on success). Maintain concentration to keep it raised or return it after. Higher: quick ascension +2d6 per slot above 5th.

## WATER — Water family
**Water** — Evocation cantrip. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Choose one or two creatures in range (if two, within 1.5 m of each other). Each makes a Dexterity save or takes 1d6 bludgeoning. Higher: +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th.
**Watera (Water II)** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Target makes a Strength save or takes 3d6 bludgeoning and falls prone or is pushed 9 m (your choice). Can instead target an unattended object up to ~90 kg (knocked over or pushed up to 9 m). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 1st.
**Waterga (Water III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. A Large-or-smaller creature makes a Dexterity save or takes 3d10 cold and is engulfed in a water prison (can't breathe, restrained, 3d10 cold at the start of each of its turns); half, no effect on success. It can use an action for a Strength save vs your DC to pop the bubble. Higher: initial +1d10 per slot above 3rd.
**Waterja (Water IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. A geyser, 6 m radius cylinder 12 m high. Creatures make a Strength save or take 6d8 bludgeoning and are thrown 6 m straight back from the nearest edge (half, no effect on success). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 5th.

## UNASPECTED — Ruin family
**Ruin** — Evocation cantrip. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Ranged spell attack; on a hit, 1d10 force. Higher: +1d10 at 5th/11th/17th.
**Ruinra (Ruin II)** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. A burst of unaspected aether, 3 m radius sphere: Dexterity save, 2d10 force (half on success). Higher: +1d10 per slot above 1st.
**Ruinga (Ruin III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. 4 bolts of energy; ranged spell attack per bolt, 2d8 force each. Higher: +1 bolt per slot above 3rd.
**Ruinja (Ruin IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. A barrage in a 6 m radius circle: Dexterity save, 4d8 force (half on success). Each turn, use your action to call it again (same or new point). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 1st (as written).

## POISON — Bio family
**Bio** — Necromancy cantrip. CT 1 action; Range 9 m; Instantaneous. Target makes a Constitution save or takes 1d4 poison and is poisoned 1 minute (extra 1d4 poison at the start of its turns; re-save at end of turn to end). Higher: initial +1d4 at 5th/11th/17th.
**Biora (Bio II)** — 1st necromancy. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Toxic mist, 3 m radius sphere: Constitution save or 4d4 poison and poisoned 1 minute (half, no poison on success; re-save at end of turn). Higher: +2d4 per slot above 1st.
**Bioga (Bio III)** — 3rd necromancy. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. Outbreak in a 4.5 m cube: Constitution save or 6d4 poison and poisoned up to 1 minute (half, no poison on success). While concentrating, use your action to spread it: unaffected creatures within 4.5 m of an affected creature must save or be affected. Re-save at end of turn. Higher: +2d4 per slot above 3rd.
**Bioja (Bio IV)** — 5th necromancy. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. A 18 m cone: Constitution save or 4d8 poison + 4d8 necrotic and poisoned 1 minute (half on success). Re-save at end of turn. Higher: necrotic +2d8 per slot above 5th.
**Biolysis** — 5th necromancy. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. Target makes a Constitution save or takes 6d10 necrotic and is poisoned (half, no effect on success). At the start of its turns it takes 1d10 necrotic and must save or spend its action retching. Re-save at end of turn to end. Higher: initial +2d10 per slot above 5th.

## NECROTIC — Dark family
**Dark** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range 27 m; Instantaneous. Ranged spell attack; on a hit, 3d8 necrotic. Higher: +1d8 per slot above 1st.
**Darkra (Dark II)** — 2nd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 9 m; Instantaneous. A 9 m cone: Dexterity save, 3d8 necrotic (half on success). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 1st (as written).
**Darkga (Dark III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. Necrotic energy beneath a target; creatures within 6 m radius make a Dexterity save, 8d6 necrotic (half on success). Higher: +1d6 per slot above 3rd.
**Darkja (Dark IV)** — 5th necromancy. CT 1 action; Range 12 m radius (Self); Instantaneous. Creatures within 12 m radius make a Charisma save, 8d8 necrotic (half on success). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 5th.

## RADIANT — Banish family
**Banish** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. Daggers of light converge; Dexterity save, 3d8 radiant (half on success). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 1st.
**Banishra (Banish II)** — 2nd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. Spears of light in a 4.5 m sphere: Dexterity save, 3d8 radiant (half on success). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 1st (as written).
**Banishga (Banish III)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range Self (9 m radius); Instantaneous. Blades of light rain on hostiles within 9 m radius: Dexterity save, 6d6 radiant (half on success). Fiends/Undead take +8 radiant (not reduced on a success). Higher: +1d6 per slot above 3rd.
**Banishja (Banish IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range Self (30 m line, 4.5 m wide); Instantaneous. Any number of creatures in the line make a Dexterity save, 8d6 radiant (half on success). Fiends/Undead take +8 radiant (not reduced on a success). Higher: +2d6 per slot above 5th.

## HEALING — Cure family
**Cure** — 1st evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. A creature regains 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier HP. No effect on undead/constructs. Higher: +1d6 per slot above 1st.
**Cura (Medica)** — 2nd evocation. CT 1 action; Range Self (6 m radius); Instantaneous. Allies within 6 m regain 1d6 + spellcasting modifier HP. No effect on undead/constructs. Higher: +1d6 per slot above 3rd (as written).
**Curada (Cure II)** — 3rd evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. A creature regains 3d8 + spellcasting modifier HP and gains 1d6 temp HP. No effect on undead/constructs. Higher: temp HP +1d6 per slot above 1st (as written).
**Curaga (Cure III)** — 4th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Instantaneous. A creature and allies within 6 m of it regain 3d6 + spellcasting modifier HP. No effect on undead/constructs. Higher: +2d6 per slot above 4th.
**Curaja (Cure IV)** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. Higher: +1d6 per slot above 5th.

## PROTECTION / UTILITY / BUFF
**Bravery** — 1st transmutation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration 1 minute. A creature's weapon attacks deal bonus damage = its proficiency bonus, and it is immune to frightened. Higher: +1 creature per slot above 1st.
**Faith** — 2nd transmutation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration 1 minute. A creature's spells deal bonus damage = its proficiency bonus, and it has advantage on Wisdom saves. Higher: +1 creature per slot above 2nd.
**Bar-Element** — 4th abjuration. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration 1 minute. Protect a creature vs one type (bludgeoning, cold, fire, lightning, necrotic, radiant, or thunder): attacks of that type vs it have disadvantage, its saves vs that type have advantage, and damage of that type is reduced by your spellcasting modifier. Higher: +1 creature, or +1 element for one creature, per slot above 3rd (as written).
**Bar-Element-ra** — 7th abjuration. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration 1 minute. As Bar-Element but the creature and allies within 6 m gain resistance to the chosen type; the named creature also gets disadvantage on attackers, advantage on its saves, and damage reduction = your spellcasting modifier. Higher: +1 creature or +1 element per slot above 7th.
**De-Element** — 4th transmutation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration 1 minute. Target makes a Wisdom save; on a failure choose a damage type — attacks of that type vs it have advantage and its saves vs that type have disadvantage. Higher: +1 creature per slot above 4th.
**Imperil** — 7th transmutation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration 1 minute. Target makes a Wisdom save or gains vulnerability to bludgeoning, cold, fire, lightning, necrotic, radiant, and thunder (immunity becomes normal damage instead).
**En-Element** — 3rd transmutation. CT 1 action; Range Touch; Concentration 1 minute. Empower a weapon with a chosen damage type (magical); on a hit, +damage = your spellcasting modifier of that type. You may use your spellcasting modifier for its attack and damage rolls. Higher: +1 weapon within 1.5 m per slot above 3rd.
**Protection** — 4th abjuration. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration 1 minute. A creature reduces bludgeoning/piercing/slashing damage by your spellcasting modifier. Higher: +1 creature per slot above 4th.
**Shell** — 4th abjuration. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Concentration 1 minute. A creature reduces all damage types except bludgeoning/piercing/slashing by your spellcasting modifier. Higher: +1 creature per slot above 4th.
**Sacred Soil** — 4th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 9 m; Concentration up to 1 minute. A 3 m radius dome; allies inside reduce damage taken by 1d6. Higher: +1d6 per slot above 4th.
**Esuna** — 3rd abjuration. CT 1 action; Range 9 m; Instantaneous. Cure a creature of blindness, deafened, paralyzed, and poisoned. Higher: +1 creature per slot above 3rd.
**Refresh** — 2nd transmutation. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; Instantaneous. A creature recovers one expended 1st-level slot. Higher: recovers a slot one level lower than the slot used to cast Refresh.
**Regen** — 4th enchantment. CT 1 action; Range 18 m; 1 minute. The target regains 1d6 HP at the start of its turn for the duration. Higher: +1d6 per slot above 4th.
**Lustrate** — 4th evocation. CT 1 action (or reaction when a creature takes damage); Range 9 m; Instantaneous. A creature regains 3d8 HP. Cannot combine with Adloquium (Nymian Scholar). Higher: +1d8 per slot above 4th.
**Tetragrammaton** — 4th evocation. CT 1 action (or reaction when a creature takes damage); Range 18 m; Instantaneous. A creature is healed 3d8 HP. Higher: +1d8 per slot above 4th.
**Assize** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range Self; Instantaneous. Allies within 18 m regain 3d8 HP; enemies within 18 m make a Wisdom save, 4d4 radiant (half on success). You may swap which dice heal and which deal damage (decide before rolling). Higher: +dice to each effect per slot above 5th.
**Asylum** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range 36 m; Concentration 1 minute. A 9 m radius globe; allies inside regain 1d8 HP at the start of their turn and gain resistance to nonmagical slashing/piercing/bludgeoning and to necrotic. Higher: +1d8 per slot above 5th.
**Collective Unconscious** — 5th evocation. CT 1 action; Range Self; Concentration 1 minute. A 9 m radius barrier; allies within 3 m heal 3d8 immediately; while inside, +1 AC and heal 1d6 at the start of their turn. Higher: +1d6 per slot above 5th.
