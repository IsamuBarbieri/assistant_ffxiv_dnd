## 2. LINEAR CAMPAIGN & D&D TTRPG DESIGN
- **Linear Progression:** The campaign follows a single, linear MSQ progression. Avoid branched or parallel fetch chores.
- **TTRPG Adaptation:** Strip out MMO-specific mechanics that make no sense in D&D (gear-level inspection tutorials, chat-box slash emote tests, video-game interface unlock banners like 'unlocks Return', role training halls like 'Hall of the Novice'). Translate necessary in-world actions into natural roleplay, dialogue, or skill checks (e.g. Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion).
- **Aetheryte Attunement:** Maintained as an in-world diegetic recommendation/advice given to new adventurers by their guild; players decide whether to attune.
- **[REC] Content Realm-Wide Reach:** Recommended optional content (optional dungeons, Hildibrand, trials) represents realm-wide news and guild bulletins accessible via /voci at any major settlement once its MSQ prerequisite is met.
